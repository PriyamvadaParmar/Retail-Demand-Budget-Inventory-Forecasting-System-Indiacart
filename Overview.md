# Indiacart – Retail Demand, Budget & Inventory Forecasting System

##  Project Overview
This project presents an end-to-end **data-driven analytics solution** built for **Indiacart**, a hypothetical e-commerce retail company.  
The solution helps improve **demand forecasting, inventory planning, and budget control** by integrating **Excel, SQL, Tableau, and Python**.

The project simulates a real-world retail environment and demonstrates how analytics can support **data-driven decision-making, operational efficiency, and revenue optimization**.

---

## Business Problem
Indiacart faces common challenges experienced by growing e-commerce companies:
- Overstocking of slow-moving products  
- Stock-outs during peak and seasonal demand periods  
- Budget overruns due to inaccurate demand forecasting  

The absence of a unified analytics system limits visibility across **sales, inventory, and marketing spend**, resulting in inefficiencies and reactive decision-making.

---

##  Project Objectives
- Forecast product demand to support proactive inventory planning  
- Monitor inventory health and identify slow-moving or high-risk SKUs  
- Track budget vs actual spend for marketing and procurement  
- Provide executive-level dashboards to support strategic decisions  

---

## Tools & Technologies Used
- **Excel** – Data preparation, pivot analysis, budgeting, forecasting, and what-if analysis  
- **SQL** – Data modeling, joins, aggregations, and analytical queries  
- **Tableau** – Interactive dashboards and KPI storytelling  
- **Python** – Time-series forecasting and predictive analytics  

---

## Data Sources (Simulated)
The project uses **simulated retail data for Indiacart**, structured across the following datasets:

- Orders & Sales Data  
- Product & Pricing Data  
- Inventory Levels  
- Marketing Budget & Spend  

The dataset spans multiple months to enable **trend, seasonality, and forecasting analysis**.

---

## Key KPIs & Metrics
- Monthly and category-wise revenue  
- Forecast vs actual sales  
- Inventory turnover ratio  
- Slow-moving and stock-out risk products  
- Budget utilization and variance analysis  
- Marketing spend effectiveness  

---

## Project Phases & Deliverables

### Phase 1: Excel – Business Analysis Foundation
- Pivot tables for category and sales performance  
- Budget vs actual analysis  
- Demand forecasting using Excel forecasting functions  
- What-if analysis for pricing and demand scenarios  

### Phase 2: SQL – Analytical Querying
- Revenue and sales trend analysis  
- Identification of slow-moving and fast-moving products  
- Inventory turnover and stock risk analysis  
- Budget variance and seasonality analysis  

### Phase 3: Tableau – Executive Dashboards
- Executive overview dashboard  
- Inventory risk and optimization dashboard  
- Budget consumption and forecast dashboard  
- Interactive filters for category, region, and time period  

### Phase 4: Python – Advanced Analytics
- Time-series decomposition  
- Demand forecasting using ARIMA / Prophet  
- Six-month sales and demand predictions  
- Budget overshoot risk identification  

---

##  Key Insights (Sample)
- Certain product categories exhibit strong seasonal demand patterns  
- A small percentage of SKUs contribute disproportionately to overstock risk  
- Marketing spend effectiveness varies significantly across categories  
- Forecast-driven planning reduces both stock-outs and excess inventory  

---

##  Business Recommendations
- Adopt forecast-driven inventory replenishment for high-risk categories  
- Reduce capital lock-in by proactively managing slow-moving SKUs  
- Align marketing budgets with demand forecasts  
- Use predictive insights to support strategic plan
